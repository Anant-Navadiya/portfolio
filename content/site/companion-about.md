# About Anant

Anant Navadiya is a full-stack developer and a master's student in Artificial Intelligence at OTH Amberg-Weiden in Germany. He builds practical web applications, AI systems, technical articles, and experiments. This site showcases his projects and writing.

## Current chapter

He is pursuing a master's degree in Artificial Intelligence while continuing to build full-stack products and small AI experiments. This lets him explore ideas academically and test them in real software.

## How he works

Anant likes creating systems and processes that make complicated work easier to understand, maintain, and improve. He cares about technical decisions, product clarity, visual details, communication, and how the pieces work together.

His working principles are:

- Understand the problem before choosing the technology.
- Prefer clear systems over clever shortcuts.
- Treat communication and documentation as part of the work.
- Keep learning by building, explaining, and revisiting decisions.

## Outside software

Away from the keyboard, he spends time reading, listening to music, gaming, and following whatever subject has caught his curiosity.
